THE PROBLEM
Rules change constantly. A record 2,496 new measures in 2025, inside and outside the EU.
Labels expire. Each shipment needs a label that matches the destination's current rules.
New EU rules keep arriving. Deforestation (EUDR, from Dec 2026), packaging (PPWR, in force) and forced labour (from Dec 2027) reach EU-only sales too.
Paperwork is manual, error prone and time consuming. Per VeriPura's figures, documentation and label causes account for 39% of FDA and 40% of EU TRACES rejections.
Support misses the gap. A €20,000 voucher against a €30,000+ consulting contract buys consultant time, not shipment checking.
THE PLATFORM
One place where an exporter finds out what applies, checks that it is met, keeps proof and manages the shipment.
Built for three groups
SMEs that export rarely or never
Single market exporters who wish to expand
Established processors and distributors that want fewer blocked, delayed or returned shipments and lower document-processing labor costs
