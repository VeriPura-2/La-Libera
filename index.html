The problem

Exporting food is a rules problem before it is a sales problem

The rules keep changing, inside the EU as well as outside it.

Rules change constantly

A record 2,496 new measures in 2025, inside and outside the EU.

Labels expire

Each shipment needs a label that matches the destination's current rules.

New EU rules keep arriving

Deforestation (EUDR, from Dec 2026), packaging (PPWR, in force) and forced labour (from Dec 2027) reach EU-only sales too.

Paperwork is manual, error prone and time consuming

Per VeriPura's figures, documentation and label causes account for 39% of FDA and 40% of EU TRACES rejections.

Most producers are too small for compliance staff

Italy has 52,400 food manufacturers and 85% are micro businesses, so few can afford a dedicated export compliance person.

Support misses the gap

A €20,000 voucher against a consulting contract above €30,000 buys consultant time, not shipment checking.

The platform

Built for three groups

One platform for exporters at every stage.

SMEs that export rarely or never

Small producers with no export staff and a first shipment ahead.

The pain: no one in house knows the labels, documents and certifications a destination requires.

With Via Libera: see which rules and documents apply, check the label and papers before shipping, and reach consultants and labs when needed.

Single market exporters who wish to expand

45.5% of Italian exporters sell into just one market.

The pain: every new country brings its own labels, documents and certifications.

With Via Libera: the destination's rules and required documents, worked out from the HS code, origin and destination, with labels and certificates kept in one place, then finds importers in the new market.

Established processors and distributors

Companies that already ship regularly.

The pain: blocked, delayed or returned shipments, and staff time spent preparing and correcting paperwork.

With Via Libera: automated label and document checks, alerts on rule changes, and a verifiable record of every check, for fewer problem shipments and lower document-processing labor costs.

Tools on the platform

Everything an exporter juggles, in one place

From finding a market to getting the shipment out. The final mix of tools is to be determined.

Get ready and stay compliant

Compliance checking

Label validation, document identification and verification, incl. Organic, Halal, Kosher. By VeriPura FOOD™.

Alerts and compliance data

Rule changes and food safety alerts for your products and markets, from sources such as SGS FoodNexus.

Shipment tracking

Status of each shipment in one view.

Digital asset manager

Labels, certificates, test reports, plus packaging and supplier declarations for new EU rules, in one place.

Testing and certification

Direct links to laboratories and certification bodies when a shipment needs them.

Export consultants

Access to temporary export managers, who gain a systematic checking tool.

Start exporting or enter new markets

Market selection and landed cost

Rank target countries for your product and see duties, taxes and freight to a delivered price.

Importer discovery

Find importers and distributors for your product in the new market, with contact details. By VeriPura.

Outbound campaigns

Sequenced emails to those importers, with tracking and replies, through an integrated partner.

Label design

Design labels per market with integrated design tools. VeriPura FOOD then validates them before printing.

Funding and grants finder

Freight and customs partners

Compare and book freight forwarders and customs brokers.
